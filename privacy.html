Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Wedding Planner (https://blur7285.github.io/wedding-planner/ and its Android app) is a private family wedding planner. It is not a commercial service.

Google sign-in

Google sign-in is used only to identify you (your name and e-mail address via the openid and email scopes), so the planner can show who made each change.

Google Drive access

The app uses only the drive.file scope. This lets it read and write only the planner data file that you select (with Google's file picker) or that the app creates. It cannot see, read or change any other files in your Google Drive.

Where your data lives

Your planner data is stored in your own Google Drive (in the planner file you choose) and in your browser or phone on your own device. There are no servers operated by the developer; the website is static pages hosted on GitHub Pages.

What we do not do

The developer does not collect, store, sell, share or transfer your data or your Google user data to anyone. There are no ads, analytics or tracking. Google user data is not used for any purpose other than providing the planner features described above, and its use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can sign out in the app at any time, and you can revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Clearing the site's data in your browser (or uninstalling the app) removes the copy on your device; the planner file in your Drive stays under your control.
